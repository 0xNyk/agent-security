# Brand field guide

The identity borrows from lab notebooks and flight-recorder traces: quiet paper, black instrument ink, and one orange interruption at the boundary. It should feel inspected and handled, not futuristic.

## Name and line

- Product name: `agent-security`
- Plain description: deterministic safety gates for agent-touched repositories
- Voice: exact, calm, skeptical of absolute claims
- Avoid: military posturing, fear copy, glowing locks, shield mascots, neon circuits, and claims that a scan proves safety

## Visual tokens

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Paper | `#F3F0E8` | Primary field-note surface |
| Carbon | `#171717` | Text and diagnostic traces |
| Signal | `#F05A28` | Stops, findings, and boundary markers |
| Graphite | `#73706A` | Secondary annotation |

Use Signal sparingly. It marks interruption or risk, not decoration.
